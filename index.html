What changed and where a person decides

Problem: Lesson drafts sounded generic because the AI was being told to "write well" in the abstract instead of showing what good looked like.

What I built: I rewrote the prompt instructions, added concrete before/after examples, and cut the grader from 15 checks to 4.

Human decision: Prioritize fixes like clear image descriptions and defining unclear terminology; make judgment calls on minor details like em dashes and sign-posting.

Testing screening rules against the evidence

A short practice exercise in choosing criteria, revising a policy, and asking for missing evidence.

In this fictional recruiting scenario, learners see which profiles their rules exclude, inspect the evidence, and apply the same criteria to a new profile.

The learning-design choices

Practice first: Learners choose rules before seeing feedback, then inspect the consequences and revise.

Check the reasoning: The final profile contains relevant experience and missing evidence. The learner needs to choose what to ask next.

Evaluation: Rule choices use an authored answer key for the fictional job brief. Notes are not graded. The exercise uses scripted feedback, without AI scoring or candidate-match percentages.
