Selected work samples

Action mapping at intake

We stopped defaulting to course builds and got to the real fix faster.

Problem: The intake flow assumed training was the answer. Requests needed a quick check for the real performance gap and the smallest effective intervention.

What I built: A triage skill that mapped goal, behavior, obstacle, and intervention before any course work started.

Human decision: The team confirms the action map together and decides whether the right move is a course, product fix, doc update, something else, or a combination.

Fixing AI writing at the source

Eval output went from 15 PASS/FAIL lines to a short problem list for a human to make the final calls.

Problem: Lesson drafts sounded generic because the AI was being told to "write well" in the abstract instead of showing what good looked like.

What I built: I rewrote the prompt instructions, added concrete before/after examples, and cut the grader from 15 checks to 4.

Human decision: Prioritize fixes like clear image descriptions and defining unclear terminology; make judgment calls on minor details like em dashes and sign-posting.

Decision roles in an AI pipeline

SME input stayed labeled as input; the education team kept the decision authority.

Problem: AI note summaries could blur who had authority to decide, turning SME input into a course commitment without preserving the real decision boundary.

What I built: A "who decides what" rule in the workflow: education owned final decisions, while SME input stayed tagged as input rather than a commitment.
