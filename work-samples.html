Selected work

Customer calls to education decisions

Problem
Customer calls held useful evidence, but deciding what needed education or product review meant repeatedly sorting transcripts by hand.
Solution
I built a workflow that preserves quotes and timestamps, connects findings to research themes, and flags questions for human review. It evolved from Notion AI to a Notion agent, then a scheduled Claude Code skill.
Result
The findings helped me refocus training for a new platform on directing the AI and checking its work. A fictional walkthrough shows the evidence and review decisions.

Action mapping at intake

Problem
Training requests arrived with a course already chosen as the answer, before anyone checked the performance gap.
Solution
I built a triage skill that checks the audience, goal, evidence, and obstacles before course design. It adapts to customer education, internal L&D, and other settings.
Result
The skill drafts a recommended response and, when learning is needed, an action map for a person to confirm. Assumptions stay visible, and proposed owners are not assignments.

Fixing AI writing at the source

Problem
Lesson drafts sounded generic. The AI was told to “write well” without examples of what good looked like.
Solution
I rewrote the drafting instructions, added concrete before-and-after examples, and cut the grader from 15 checks to four.
Result
The review changed from repeated PASS/FAIL rounds to one pass with a short problem list. A person makes the final calls on what to fix.

Testing a recruiting AI workflow

Problem
Recruiting AI can miss relevant experience, overstate evidence, or act before a person checks its work.
Solution
I built a fictional practice lab with four decisions: set job-relevant criteria, check AI claims against sources, handle an access request, and plan a small pilot.
Result
The prototype provides scripted feedback and a copyable review of decisions, follow-up questions, and notes. Facilitator guidance supports live discussion and practice with a fresh example.
