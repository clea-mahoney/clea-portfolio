Launching a customer academy

Problem
Customers needed a way to learn a technical product without relying on repeated live training.
Solution
I led LMS selection and the academy launch at Rewst, replacing long recordings with self-paced courses, scenario-based assessments, and live practice.
Result
The LMS launched in 55 days. The academy reached 3,000+ active learners in its first year.

Fixing AI writing at the source

Problem
Lesson drafts sounded generic. The AI was told to “write well” without examples of what good looked like.
Solution
I rewrote the drafting instructions, added concrete before-and-after examples, and cut the grader from 15 checks to four.
Result
The review changed from repeated PASS/FAIL rounds to one pass with a short problem list. A person makes the final calls on what to fix.

Action mapping at intake

Problem
Training requests arrived with a course already chosen as the answer, before anyone checked the performance gap.
Solution
I built a triage skill that checks the audience, goal, evidence, and obstacles before course design. It adapts to customer education, internal L&D, and other settings.
Result
The skill drafts a recommended response and, when learning is needed, an action map for a person to confirm. Assumptions stay visible, and proposed owners are not assignments.

Testing screening rules against the evidence

Problem
A screening rule can exclude relevant experience or treat a matching tool or title as enough evidence.
Solution
I built a fictional exercise where learners choose rules, inspect three profiles, revise their policy, and apply it to a new profile.
Result
The working prototype gives feedback on rule choices and asks learners to identify missing evidence. Feedback follows an authored answer key for the fictional job.
