Getting started with RoboRewsty

Problem
We wanted people to find a useful first task with Rewst’s in-app AI assistant. Customers could learn techniques in Cluck University, but prospects couldn’t access the courses.
My approach
I led the team that made short demos anyone could watch on YouTube. Each linked customers to more practice in Cluck University.
What if you have an idea of what to automate but no clue where to start? In this demo, the learner asks RoboRewsty to interview them. You can follow the conversation from the first question through the finished prompt.
Result
Prospects sometimes reached out to ask for course access. Among 26 course completers with usable data, weekly conversations averaged 4.4 before training and 8.7 afterward. Across all users, RoboRewsty usage also doubled during the rollout of the AI assistant.

Teaching customers to build with AI

Problem
Customers came to the same live workshop with different levels of experience connecting AI to their tools.
My approach
I designed an opening poll so the facilitator could choose an explanation that fit the room. In this adaptation, learners review the AI’s plan and decide whether two similar-looking workflows serve different purposes.

A course prospects can try

Problem
How do you teach automation to someone who hasn’t seen the product?
My approach
I designed the public Rewst Crash Course for prospects who wanted to see how the platform worked before they had an account. I rebuilt its six-lesson sequence in Parta with fictional Flowstack examples. Learners tour the builder, trace a form value through a workflow, then run a Hello World example themselves. I added a two-question quiz to the portfolio version.

Customer calls to education decisions

Problem
Customer calls held useful evidence, but deciding what needed education or product review meant repeatedly sorting transcripts by hand.
My approach
I built a workflow that preserves quotes and timestamps, connects findings to research themes, and flags questions for human review. It evolved from Notion AI to a Notion agent, then a scheduled Claude Code skill.
Result
The findings helped me refocus training for a new platform on directing the AI and checking its work. A fictional walkthrough shows the evidence and review decisions.
